Skyrim SE mod: Doubles enchanting power and triples tempering power for two-handed weapons.

Due to the idiosyncrasies of the vanilla tempering system (and just like with the 2x bonus from Smithing perks), the actual base damage improvement from tempering may vary.

Compatibility: doesn't change any vanilla elements. Will work with any vanilla or mod-added weapon so long as it has one of the vanilla 2H weapon keywords (WeapTypeGreatsword, WeapTypeBattleaxe, or WeapTypeWarhammer).
